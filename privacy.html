Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information this website collects when you visit it or contact me, and how that information is used.

01

Introduction

This is the personal portfolio website of Antony Mwendwa, a cybersecurity specialist and developer based in Machakos, Kenya. In this policy, "I", "me" and "my" refer to Antony Mwendwa.

By using this website or sending me a message, you agree to the practices described here. This policy is written with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 in mind.

02

Information I Collect

I only collect what I need to reply to you and keep the site running.

Personal Information

When you use the contact form, you give me:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The subject of your message
  • The content of your message

Automated Data Collection

When you visit the site, the hosting server may record:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser and device type
  • Pages visited and time of visit
  • The website that referred you
03

How I Use Your Information

Your information is used only for the following purposes:

Replying to your messages and enquiries
Preparing quotes and proposals you ask for
Delivering services you have agreed to
Keeping the website secure and available
Detecting and blocking spam and abuse
Meeting legal and tax obligations
04

Information Sharing and Disclosure

I do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. It is only shared in these cases:

With Your Consent

If you ask me to work with another person or company on your project, I will only share the details they need, and only after you agree.

Legal Requirements

I may disclose information when the law requires it, including:

  • A valid court order or legal process
  • A lawful request from a government authority
  • Protecting my rights, safety or property
  • Investigating fraud or security incidents
05

Data Security

As a security professional, I take the protection of your information seriously:

Encryption

All traffic to this site is encrypted with HTTPS.

Access Control

Only I can access messages sent through this site.

Data Protection

Messages are deleted once they are no longer needed.

06

Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to control your personal information. You can ask me to:

Access Your Data

Get a copy of the information I hold about you.

Correct Information

Fix any details that are wrong or out of date.

Delete Your Data

Remove your information when it is no longer needed.

Object to Processing

Ask me to stop using your information for a purpose.

07

Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. When I do, the date at the top of this page will change.

Please check this page occasionally. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
